Industrial Painting & Coating

Protecting your industrial assets from harsh environmental degradation is critical. We offer comprehensive surface preparation, abrasive blasting, and high-performance industrial coating applications.

Abrasive Sandblasting

High-pressure grit, garnet, and sand blasting to achieve specific surface profiles (SA 2.5/SA 3.0).

Protective Epoxies & Polyurethanes

Application of multi-coat heavy duty industrial paint systems for steel structures and storage tanks.

Intumescent Fireproofing

Specialized passive fire protection coatings designed to preserve structural integrity under extreme heat.

Internal Pipe Coating

Specialized internal lining and anti-corrosion applications for process piping and water transmission lines.

NACE/BGAS Inspection

Rigorous climatic monitoring, WFT/DFT testing, holiday detection, and adhesion testing by certified inspectors.

Eco-Friendly Water Blasting

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Quality control is the cornerstone of industrial safety. Our NDT division provides comprehensive testing and inspection services to detect surface and sub-surface defects in welds, pipelines, and structural steel.

Radiographic Testing (RT)

X-ray and Gamma-ray inspection for deep volumetric flaw detection in critical welds and castings.

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

High-frequency sound wave analysis for precise thickness gauging and internal defect sizing.

Magnetic Particle Inspection

Rapid surface and near-surface crack detection for ferromagnetic materials (MPI/MT).

Liquid Penetrant Testing

Dye penetrant inspection (DPI/PT) for identifying surface-breaking defects in non-porous materials.

Phased Array UT (PAUT)

Advanced multi-element ultrasonic scanning for complex geometries and faster inspection rates.

API & Plant Integrity

Comprehensive storage tank, piping, and pressure vessel inspections as per API 650/653 and API 510.
