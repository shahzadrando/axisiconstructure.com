Geotechnical & Water

Geotechnical, Piling & Water Infrastructure

Geotechnical Engineering & Piling

The stability of any massive structure begins deep underground. Our geotechnical division specializes in complex deep foundation systems, retaining walls, and soil improvement techniques.

Bored Cast-In-Situ Piling

Large-diameter rotary drilling and continuous flight auger (CFA) piling for heavy skyscraper foundations.

Secant & Contiguous Walls

Construction of interlocking pile walls for deep basement excavation and earth retention.

Sheet Piling & Shoring

Vibro-driving of interlocking steel sheet piles for temporary trenches, cofferdams, and marine bulkheads.

Soil Improvement

Vibro-compaction, stone columns, and dynamic compaction to stabilize loose or reclaimed soils.

Geotechnical Investigation

Deep soil boring, core sampling, and comprehensive laboratory analysis prior to engineering design.

Pile Load Testing

Dynamic (PDA) and static load testing to verify ultimate bearing capacity and pile integrity.

Water & Wastewater Treatment

AXIS Icon Structure plays a vital role in water security by constructing state-of-the-art desalination plants (RO), sewage treatment plants (STP), and industrial effluent treatment facilities.

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plants

Mechanical installation of high-pressure pumps, membrane racks, and energy recovery devices.

Sewage Treatment (STP)

Construction of aeration tanks, clarifiers, and installation of biological treatment systems.

GRP & GRE Piping

Specialized fiberglass pipe jointing, lamination, and installation for highly corrosive seawater intake lines.

Concrete Water Reservoirs

Civil construction of massive, leak-proof subterranean and elevated concrete water storage tanks.

Industrial Effluent Treatment

Design-build solutions for neutralizing and treating toxic chemical runoff from manufacturing plants.

Pumping Stations

Turnkey construction of massive municipal water distribution and lift pumping stations.
