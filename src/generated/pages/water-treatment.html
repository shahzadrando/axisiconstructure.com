Overview

Desalination & Treatment Infrastructure

AXIS Icon Structure plays a vital role in water security by constructing state-of-the-art desalination plants (RO), sewage treatment plants (STP), and industrial effluent treatment facilities.

Our multi-disciplinary teams handle the massive concrete basin construction, complex GRP/FRP pipe networks, and precision installation of massive pumps and filtration membranes required for continuous water processing.

Core Expertise

Key Capabilities

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plants

Mechanical installation of high-pressure pumps, membrane racks, and energy recovery devices.

Sewage Treatment (STP)

Construction of aeration tanks, clarifiers, and installation of biological treatment systems.

GRP & GRE Piping

Specialized fiberglass pipe jointing, lamination, and installation for highly corrosive seawater intake lines.

Concrete Water Reservoirs

Civil construction of massive, leak-proof subterranean and elevated concrete water storage tanks.

Industrial Effluent Treatment

Design-build solutions for neutralizing and treating toxic chemical runoff from manufacturing plants.

Pumping Stations

Turnkey construction of massive municipal water distribution and lift pumping stations.
