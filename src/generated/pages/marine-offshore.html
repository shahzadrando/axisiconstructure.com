Overview

Offshore Infrastructure & Marine Engineering

AXIS Icon Structure extends its engineering prowess into the ocean, offering specialized construction, maintenance, and modification services for offshore oil & gas platforms, FPSOs, and coastal infrastructure.

Our offshore-certified workforce (BOSIET/HUET) is trained to execute complex mechanical, structural, and electrical scopes in highly restrictive and hazardous marine environments.

Core Expertise

Key Capabilities

Platform Modifications

Brownfield structural upgrades, deck extensions, and topside module integration for existing platforms.

Marine Jetty Construction

Civil and structural development of marine jetties, loading terminals, and coastal docking facilities.

Subsea Spool Fabrication

Specialized onshore fabrication of subsea manifolds, tie-in spools, and heavy risers.

FPSO Maintenance

Onboard mechanical overhauls, piping replacements, and tank cleaning for Floating Production units.

Rope Access (IRATA)

Deployment of IRATA-certified rope access technicians for difficult-to-reach offshore inspections and repairs.

Offshore Wind Integration

Structural foundations, cabling, and substation support for offshore renewable energy farms.
