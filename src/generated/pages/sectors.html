Heavy Industry

Oil, Gas, Petrochemical & Fertilizer

Oil & Refinery Department

AXIS ICON STRUCTURE operates a dedicated Oil & Refinery department equipped to manage high-stakes, technically complex refinery projects. We support major refinery operators from ground-up installations to critical maintenance shutdowns.

High-Pressure Piping

Fabrication, exotic alloy welding (stainless, duplex, chrome-moly), and installation of high-pressure utility and process piping networks.

Equipment Installation

Rigging and placement of static and rotating equipment including distillation columns, reactors, pressure vessels, and large heat exchangers.

Shutdowns & Turnarounds

24/7 rapid maintenance support, mechanical cleaning, valve replacements, and internal column overhauls during scheduled plant turnarounds.

Hazardous E&I Works

Intrinsically safe electrical distribution, transmitter installation, control loop testing, and transmitter calibration within hazardous Zones.

Specialized Manpower

Provision of certified high-pressure welders, structural fitters, instrument technicians, QA/QC inspectors, and safety officers.

Safety Compliance (HSE)

Rigorous risk assessment, permit-to-work (PTW) coordination, LOTO procedures, and full compliance with refinery safety audits.

Petrochemical Department

AXIS ICON STRUCTURE operates a specialized Petrochemical department focused on chemical processing plant developments. Our expertise spans heavy structural assembly, exotic polymer piping, and hazardous-area E&I automation.

Polymer & Chemical Piping

Fabrication and welding of specialized double-walled piping, jacketed lines, and high-purity chemical transit lines.

DCS & Automation Control

Installation and loop testing of Distributed Control Systems (DCS), Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC), and process automation safety networks.

Modular Skid Assembly

In-shop fabrication and site integration of modular chemical process skids, pre-assembled structural units, and pumps.

Tank Farms & Storage

Civil foundation piling, structural tank erection, and internal chemical-resistant lining for storage facilities.

Manpower & Site Support

Certified instrument technicians, industrial safety officers, experienced piping engineers, and technical supervisors.

Industrial Safety & HSE

Risk mitigation, toxic gas monitoring setup, chemical protective protocols, and strict adherence to OSHA and plant safety standards.

Fertilizer Department

AXIS ICON STRUCTURE operates a dedicated Fertilizer department focused on heavy mechanical maintenance and plant outfitting. We support major ammonia and urea manufacturing units with specialized services during expansions and shutdown turnarounds.

Primary Reformer Maintenance

Catalyst tube extraction and welding, burner overhauls, refractory repairs, and manifold replacements on primary reformers.

Ammonia & Urea Piping

High-integrity piping fabrication in high-pressure loops, corrosive environments, and alloy-dependent process lines.

Heavy Rigging & Erection

Heavy lift engineering and placement of massive vessels, CO2 absorbers, urea reactors, and high-capacity centrifugal compressors.

E&I Process Controls

Instrumentation calibration, loop checking, gas detection transmitters installation, and testing of process control loops.

ASME Certified Manpower

Provision of ASME Section IX certified high-alloy welders, heavy rigging specialists, safety inspectors, and quality supervisors.

Scaffolding & Access

Certified heavy-duty scaffolding erection for reformer towers, storage tanks, and high-elevation industrial structures.
