Overview

Complex Piping Networks & Fabrication

AXIS Icon Structure provides specialized fabrication and installation of complex industrial piping systems and cross-country pipelines for the oil & gas, petrochemical, and water sectors.

Our ASME-certified welders and fabricators work with carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex, and exotic alloys to ensure leak-proof, high-pressure containment under the most extreme operating conditions.

Core Expertise

Key Capabilities

In-Shop Pipe Fabrication

Controlled environment fabrication of complex pipe spools using advanced automated welding techniques.

Cross-Country Pipelines

Large-scale trenching, stringing, welding, and lowering of transmission pipelines across challenging terrains.

Exotic Alloy Welding

Specialized TIG/MIG welding procedures for Super Duplex, Inconel, and Titanium piping systems.

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

In-house coordination of Radiographic Testing (RT), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), and Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI).

Hydrostatic Testing

High-pressure water/pneumatic testing of completed spools and pipeline segments to guarantee integrity.

Hot Tapping & Tie-Ins

Live pipeline connections and complex tie-ins into existing operational facilities without shutting down production.
